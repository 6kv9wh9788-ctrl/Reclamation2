# Approved world-life direction — 2026-09-28

The map scale is approved. The world should gain regionally distinct architecture, settlement layouts inspired by historical towns, scheduled civilian life, day/night, weather and ecologically placed detail. Cultural references should use specific traditions and periods rather than one generic representation of an entire continent. Future faction kits should differ in layout, materials, roof forms, civic buildings and defenses, while preserving readable building roles.

Rare valuable wilderness creatures should exist in the world and be discoverable through imperfect scout reports with location, sighting time and threat/capture assessments. One requested creature has a giraffe body, horse legs, bear head, cat ears, shark teeth and bunny tail. Its design and encounter remain a subsequent milestone, not part of the inhabited-village build.

Selective enterable civic buildings may support contested occupation objectives. Entering a hall alone must not silently award a village or bypass defenders. Civilian settlements and military facilities keep separate development paths. The first slice provides hall entry and interior space only; conquest conditions remain future work.

## Procedural variation

The user explicitly requires variation in generated worlds, settlements and progression, weather, encounters and other appropriate systems. Avoid repeating an identical town/city/fortress layout everywhere. Use reproducible seeds and independent random streams so cosmetic changes cannot reshuffle combat outcomes or strategic state. Random choices need constraints: accessible entrances, traversable routes, non-overlapping buildings and intelligible settlement structure.

Variation should be conditioned on terrain, resources, culture, population, technology and previous development rather than unconstrained random placement or upgrades. Combat variation should initially concern enemies, encounter composition and decisions; the existing approved player attack/dodge contract remains protected.

Current implementation scope: stable per-site seeds vary village starting plans and preview placements on the two existing atlas maps. This is not yet a general terrain generator, dynamic settlement-growth simulation, weather system or randomized combat implementation. First establish and verify one usable village system before integrating the remaining layers.
