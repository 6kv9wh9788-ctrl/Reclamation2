# Proposed Companion AI 2.0 sprint

Status: authorized after onboarding and implemented on 2026-09-25; Tom subsequently reported a successful manual playtest. The report does not enumerate every acceptance case. The separate Company staging repair is documented in COMPANY_STAGING_REPAIR.md. See [COMPANION_AI_2_RESULTS.md](COMPANION_AI_2_RESULTS.md) for delivered behavior, executed tests, the original baseline defect and the subsequent manual playtest report. The scope and acceptance criteria below are retained as the sprint agreement.

## Scope

Improve the two ordinary companions (Mara and Bren) in Squad and Hulk scenarios: maintain useful weapon-dependent spacing, respond reliably to visible Hulk tells, and introduce bounded wounded/exhausted recovery under existing orders. Reuse the existing threat/defense and recovery concepts. Company soldiers, Gateway/Horde adaptive defense, enemy AI, player combat and visuals stay outside this first sprint. Shared helper changes require regression coverage for all callers; prefer changes local to ordinary companion decisions.

Existing behavior already includes opposing flank stations, threat prioritization, obstacle-aware escape choices, stamina reserves, order leashes, and committed-action protection. Recovery/cover/fallback already exist in tactical and company policies. First add characterization scenarios to identify which ordinary-squad cases actually need improvement; do not replace working behavior solely to satisfy a new architecture preference.

## Deliverables and measurable acceptance

1. **Spacing:** in a fixed Squad fixture with stationary hero/target and sword/spear companions, settle within 3 simulated seconds. Over the following 10 seconds, with active fighting, no companion/player body overlap beyond 0.05 m tolerance; companion-to-companion distance remains >1 m in unobstructed positioning; spear preferred range remains at least 0.35 m farther than sword. An obstructed fixture must avoid terrain penetration and report blocked routing rather than teleport. Explicitly isolate dodge/interruption periods when evaluating settled formation ranges.
2. **Telegraphs:** parameterize Hulk Sweep and Smash at least over two companion approach directions and clear/blocked escape cases. A ready companion with >=25 stamina and a feasible escape must initiate a paid dodge before impact when the configured tell window is reached; the clear-path fixture must avoid the resolved strike. A blocked or exhausted companion uses legal guard/fallback behavior, never a free dodge. A committed companion must finish its action rather than cancel into defense. Assert outcomes at the real impact event, not merely an intent label.
3. **Recovery:** propose ordinary-companion entry at <=35% health or <30 stamina, with stamina recovery hysteresis to 65, matching existing tactical concepts. On the next eligible decision within 0.35 seconds, stop offensive pursuit and choose a safe position within the current order leash. No automatic healing, invulnerability or global retreat. Recovery must not oscillate each step around thresholds; wounded companions only take an explicitly tested safe opening. This adds behavior to ordinary companions and needs playtest approval before release.
4. **Orders:** preserve Follow/Hold/Assault leashes (7/3/14 m). Withdraw clears offensive targets, finishes the committed action and starts no fresh attack while returning. Retain the existing test demonstrating >2 m camp-distance reduction in 3 seconds. Hold returns within 0.6 m of its original anchor in the existing unobstructed fixture. Pause/reset/target death must clear or freeze the appropriate transient state.
5. **Player protection:** unchanged player combat/profile/input/camera code and serialized assets; all attack/dodge, weapon, sprint, guard/poise, limb and feedback regressions pass. Run complete Reclamation EditMode and PlayMode suites and report all pre-existing failures separately. Do not weaken existing tests or retune player mechanics to compensate for companion behavior.
6. **Determinism and review:** replay fixed fixtures three times at 1/60-second steps with identical decision transitions and outcomes. Capture concise intent/reason/target/recovery diagnostics using existing test seams. Conduct a manual Duel/Weapons control comparison plus Squad/Hulk playtest; Tom confirms player attack/dodge feel is unchanged and companion retreat is not excessive.

## Boundaries and sequencing

Start only after onboarding failures, if any, are triaged and the in-progress Sidekick work is kept as an explicit baseline. Implement characterization tests, then the smallest ordinary-companion policy changes, then run regressions and manual review. Do not add a behavior-tree framework, navigation replacement, revival, equipment changes, new art, commander redesign, new company tactics, persistence, or general combat rebalance. Thresholds above are proposed acceptance targets, not claims about current passing behavior.
